Cachet for Mac

Need help? Email support@nicoleslittlebrother.tech and include your macOS version. We usually reply within two working days.

I removed something by mistake

Cachet only ever moves items to the Trash. Open the Trash in Finder, select the item and choose File › Put Back.

Why does Cachet need my home folder?

Apps from the App Store can only see folders you choose. Your home folder holds the caches, developer files and downloads Cachet looks at. Cachet reads names and sizes only, never what’s inside your files.

Cachet asked for my home folder again

This can happen after moving your home folder or restoring from a backup. Choose it once more and Cachet will remember it.

Why can’t Cachet remove simulators, Docker or Time Machine snapshots?

macOS doesn’t let App Store apps change these. Cachet shows how much space they use and the exact step that frees it, such as a Terminal command you can copy, or the right page in System Settings.

Restoring your purchase

Choose Cachet › Restore Purchase… on any Mac signed in with the same Apple Account.
